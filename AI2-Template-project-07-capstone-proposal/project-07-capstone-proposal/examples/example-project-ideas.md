## Choosing Your Project

### Selection Criteria Checklist

- [ ] Does it solve a REAL problem you've observed?
- [ ] Can you explain WHO benefits and HOW?
- [ ] Does it use AI/ML concepts from the course?
- [ ] Is it feasible with available tools and time?
- [ ] Is the scope appropriate (not too broad/narrow)?
- [ ] Can you get data for testing?

### Narrowing Down

1. List 3 problems you care about
2. For each, ask: "Can AI help solve this?"
3. Identify which AI technique fits best
4. Discuss feasibility with teammates
5. Choose the one with clearest path to implementation

---

## Questions to Ask Yourself

1. **Who is affected?** Can you name specific people or groups?
2. **How big is the problem?** Can you find statistics?
3. **What exists already?** What are the limitations of current solutions?
4. **Why AI?** Would a simpler solution work? AI should add clear value.
5. **What data do you need?** Is it available or can you collect it?

---

Remember: The best projects solve problems you personally understand and care about!
